About me

I'm a PhD candidate and sessional lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Victoria, Canada.

What I work on

My research connects computer graphics, computer-aided design, and machine learning. I am especially interested in geometric representations, B-Rep models, 3D point clouds, and language-guided reasoning about the structure and materials of 3D objects.

I work under the supervision of Dr. Teseo Schneider ↗.
