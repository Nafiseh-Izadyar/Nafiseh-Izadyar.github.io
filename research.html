Selected work

Research

My research focuses on making 3D geometry more accessible, useful, and interpretable, from CAD representations to language-guided material understanding.

01 / CAD representations

Better STEP

Better STEP introduces an open, HDF5-based representation of boundary representation (B-Rep) geometry and an associated dataset and Python library. It makes CAD geometry and topology easier to query, sample, and use in learning pipelines.

02 / Multimodal 3D understanding

LLM-Guided Material Inference

A two-stage, zero-shot approach to assigning plausible materials to parts of 3D point clouds. The model first reasons about object semantics, then uses that context to infer material compositions for segmented parts.

A method that preserves the topology encoded in boundary-representation CAD models throughout the meshing process, while controlling geometric approximation with a user-defined tolerance.
